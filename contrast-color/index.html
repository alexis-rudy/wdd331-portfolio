contrast-color Demo

This is a demo of the contrast-color library.

I am a black box with white text. The contrast-color library automatically sets the text color to white for good contrast.
I am a white box with black text. The contrast-color library automatically sets the text color to black for good contrast.
I am a box with lightness of 40%. The contrast-color library automatically sets the text color to black for good contrast, but it struggles because the background is too light.
